How are the groups defined?

Clear coalition: Likud, Otzma Yehudit, Shas, UTJ, RZP–Zehut and Amcha Yisrael. Clear opposition: Yashar, Together, Democrats, Yisrael Beiteinu and Reservists–Economic. Others: Joint List, Ra’am and currently unassigned minor lists. These are scenario classifications, not confirmed future governing alliances.

These are model-based uncertainty ranges, not conformal confidence intervals with validated 90% coverage. They depend on the assumed vote shares and volatility. The 61-seat line marks a parliamentary majority.

Data connection / optional reliable proxy

The website first requests the same-origin server’s /api/turnout endpoint. If the source is unavailable it may try browser-side mirrors, which can fail due to site access controls. A missing feed is not treated as fresh data.

Only explicitly parsed national turnout will update. An unreachable source will not be labeled fresh.
