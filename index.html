Data connection / optional reliable proxy

The official CEC map above is the primary public display. In addition, this page independently tries to retrieve the Mako media mirror of national turnout using browser-accessible relays; those requests are best-effort and may fail due to CORS or access policies. For a dependable national media mirror, deploy the included mako-worker.js on a Cloudflare Worker and paste its HTTPS URL here.

Only explicitly parsed national turnout will update. An unreachable source will not be labeled fresh.
